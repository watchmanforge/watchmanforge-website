How it works

One day in the Forge

A few minutes, spread across the day. Built for the tired days too.

  1. Morning

    Pick today’s watch

    A short list of what matters today. Not your whole life. Just today.

  2. During the day

    Held or Conquered

    Bad day? Do the minimum and it’s Held. Good day? Go all the way and it’s Conquered. Either way, the day counts.

  3. At the time you pick

    Your phone rings

    The Scribe calls and runs your check-in by voice. You just answer and talk. Free Android

  4. Evening

    The nightly replay

    Look back at the day as a spectator watching the player, not a judge handing down a sentence.

  5. Over the weeks

    Habits become armor

    Hold a habit five times and it’s tempered into a piece of armor in your Armory. Slip, and it dulls, but it’s never deleted. Three holds re-temper it.

    → Tempered

The one rule

Never miss
twice.

Missing a day happens. Missing two is how projects quietly die. The whole app is built around that one line.

ADHD-friendly by design, not by label. Short lists. A real call instead of one more notification. Room to slip without losing everything.

Privacy

Your records stay yours.

An accountability app sees your real days. You deserve a straight answer about where they go.

  • We never sell your data.

    Not to advertisers, not to anyone. And no ads in anything you pay for.

  • Your calls stay with the Scribe.

    Calls are never sold, never shared and never used to train AI. The audio is deleted once it’s turned into text, and your check-ins are stored encrypted, for you only.

  • Outside lookups go through the Forge.

    When the Forge looks something up for you, the request goes out through us, so the outside service deals with the Forge, not with you.

  • This site practices it too.

    No cookies, no analytics, no ad pixels. The waitlist never touches Google. Read the plain-words privacy policy.

Questions

Straight answers

Is it on iPhone?

Not yet. Watchman Forge is Android first. Join the waitlist and we’ll tell you when there’s news on iPhone.

What does it cost?

The Watch is free. Forever. The Scribe’s call is free too. Paid plans come later, and Founding Knights hear about them first.

Is this an ADHD app?

It’s an app for finishing what you start. It’s ADHD-friendly by design, not by label: short lists, a real call instead of one more notification, and room to slip without losing everything. It isn’t a medical product and it doesn’t treat anything.

Do you sell my data?

No. Your records stay yours. We don’t sell data, and there are no ads in anything you pay for. The privacy policy has the details in plain words.
