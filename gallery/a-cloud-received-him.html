A Cloud Received Him

Two men in white ask why they are still looking at the sky.

“And when he had spoken these things, while they beheld, he was taken up; and a cloud received him out of their sight.”

Acts 1:9 · King James Version, quoted verbatim

The Scene

The last thing the disciples see is him going up, and then the cloud takes him — the same cloud that filled the tabernacle and covered Sinai, the visible weight of the presence of God.

They are still staring upward when two men in white clothing ask them why. He will come back the same way he left. Until then the watch is theirs, and the book of Acts is what they did with it.
