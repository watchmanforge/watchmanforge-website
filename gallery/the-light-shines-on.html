The Light Shines On

The fire goes up. The man on the steps is left in the dark.

“And the light shineth in darkness; and the darkness comprehended it not.”

John 1:5 · King James Version, quoted verbatim

The Scene

John opens his gospel with the whole story compressed into one line of physics: light in darkness, and the darkness could not take hold of it. The word behind comprehended carries both senses at once — the dark neither understood the light nor overcame it.

The plate is Elijah's ascent: the chariot going up in a whirlwind and Elisha flat against the stone below, watching his master leave. The light does not go out when it leaves the frame. It goes on.
