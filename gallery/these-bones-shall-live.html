These Bones Shall Live

Set down in a valley of bones, and told to preach to them.

“Thus saith the Lord GOD unto these bones; Behold, I will cause breath to enter into you, and ye shall live:”

Ezekiel 37:5 · King James Version, quoted verbatim

The Scene

The prophet is carried out and set down in the middle of a valley full of bones — very many, and very dry. God asks him whether they can live, and Ezekiel gives the only honest answer available: O Lord GOD, thou knowest.

Then he is told to prophesy to them. There is a noise, and a shaking, and bone comes to bone; sinew, flesh, skin, and finally breath. Israel in exile had said their hope was lost and they were cut off. This was the reply.
