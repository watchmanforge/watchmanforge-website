Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

The short version

  • We don’t sell your data. Not to advertisers, not to anyone.
  • This website has no cookies, no analytics and no ad trackers. Fonts and images are served from our own site, so your browser doesn’t contact Google or anyone else to show you these pages.
  • You join the waitlist by emailing us at watchmanforge@gmail.com. We keep your address on the list and use it only to tell you about Watchman Forge.
  • Your records in the app stay yours. No ads in anything you pay for.
  • The Scribe’s calls are never sold, shared or used to train AI. Call audio is deleted once it’s turned into text, and your check-ins are stored encrypted, for you only.
  • You can ask us to see, export or delete what we hold about you at any time: email watchmanforge@gmail.com.

1. Who we are

Watchman Forge makes the Watchman Forge app and runs watchmanforge.com. We’re based in New Jersey, USA. In this policy, “we” and “us” means Watchman Forge, and “you” means anyone who visits the site, joins the waitlist, emails us or uses the app.

2. This website

We don’t collect anything about you when you browse this site. There are no cookies, no analytics tools, no advertising pixels and no social media trackers.

Like every website, this one is delivered by a hosting company: ours is Vercel. To send you a page and protect the site from abuse, Vercel’s servers handle the technical details every web request carries, such as your IP address, your browser type and the page you asked for. That’s covered by Vercel’s privacy policy. We don’t use those details to identify or profile you.

The Gallery links to our shop on Etsy. If you buy a print, that happens on Etsy under Etsy’s privacy policy. We see the order details Etsy shares with sellers, and we use them only for that order.

3. The waitlist

You join the waitlist by sending an email to watchmanforge@gmail.com. We get your email address and whatever you write, and we keep your address on our waitlist. Our mailbox is hosted by Google (Gmail), so Google handles those messages under its privacy policy.

  • What we send: your early-access invitation, launch news, and the occasional short note about how the app is coming along.
  • Tracking: none. Our emails carry no open or click tracking.
  • Founding Knights: the first 100 people to email us to join become Founding Knights. We use the order the emails arrive in only to work that out.
  • Leaving: reply and ask, and we’ll take you off the list and delete your address.

4. Emailing us

If you email us, we get your email address and whatever you write. Our mailbox is hosted by Google (Gmail), so Google handles those messages under its privacy policy. We keep messages as long as we need them to help you, and we’ll delete them if you ask.

5. The app

The Watchman Forge app is in early access. These promises hold now and will keep holding:

  • Your records stay yours. Your lists, check-ins, replays, Armory, Pantry photos, shelves and notes are used to run the app for you.
  • We don’t sell your data, and there are no ads in anything you pay for.
  • Outside lookups go through the Forge. When the app looks something up for you, the request goes out through our servers, so the outside service deals with the Forge, not with you.
  • The Reliquary is encrypted.
  • The Scribe’s call needs your phone number so it can ring you at the time you choose, and it listens to what you say on the call to run your check-in. The call is never sold, never shared and never used to train AI. The audio is deleted once it’s been turned into text, and your check-ins are stored encrypted, for you only.

Before early access opens to the waitlist, we’ll add to this section the full list of services that handle app data, what each one does, and how long data is kept.

6. Children

Watchman Forge isn’t meant for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect personal information from them. If you think a child under 13 has given us information, email us and we’ll delete it.

7. Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to:

  • show you what personal information we hold about you;
  • correct it;
  • give you a copy you can take elsewhere;
  • delete it.

Email watchmanforge@gmail.com and we’ll answer within 30 days. We won’t treat you any differently for asking.

California residents: under the CCPA you have the right to know, delete and correct your personal information, and to opt out of its sale or sharing. We don’t sell or share personal information as the CCPA defines those terms, so there’s nothing to opt out of.

8. Keeping it safe

We use the security our providers offer (encrypted connections on every page, and access limited to the people who need it), and we keep as little as we can. No system is perfect, so if something ever goes wrong that affects your data, we’ll tell you.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date at the top. If a change affects how we use your waitlist email, we’ll email you about it first.

10. Contact

Questions or requests: watchmanforge@gmail.com. Watchman Forge, New Jersey, USA.
