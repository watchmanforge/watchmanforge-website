Know what it’s worth. From real sold prices.

Add the things you collect and the app keeps a live value on each one, built from sold prices first, and labels it plainly when nothing has sold. Set a selling rule and it tells you when an item is ready to sell.

  • Sold prices first. When nothing has sold, it falls back to a price guide, then to asking prices, and says so.
  • Ready to sell: it flags the items that hit your selling rule, and the prices that are spiking.
  • Missing a piece? Note what’s missing and see what it would be worth complete; the category menu gathers them under Missing a piece.
  • Coming: a card scanner, and naming an item from a photo. Coming
