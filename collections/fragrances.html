The atomizer

It counts the sprays, then learns the bottle.

One tap a day is all it asks. The app does the arithmetic, so the last spray never takes you by surprise.

  1. A fair first guess

    Every bottle starts at 0.1 ml a spray and 5 sprays a wear. Change the sprays to match how you wear it.

  2. It learns your bottle

    After 10 sprays, set the level by eye once. The app compares what you see with what it counted, and learns how much this bottle really gives.

  3. Always knows what’s left

    Millilitres, sprays and wears left, on every bottle. Body sprays are counted in cans.

The fragrance assistant

It only picks from your shelf.

Ask what to wear and it chooses from the bottles you already own.

  • Only bottles you own. It never suggests something to buy.
  • Skips today’s bottle. What you already wore today stays off the list.
  • Leans on the season, and on the bottle you’ve gone longest without.

An honest note on pheromones

Some fragrances are sold on the promise of pheromones. The science on human pheromones is unproven, and the app says so plainly instead of repeating the promise.
